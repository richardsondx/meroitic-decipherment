# U3 retest: Rilly-merge newly covered forms

## Result

- The merge covered 178 new tokens in the royal narratives.
- The split is 65 in REM 1044 and 41 in REM 1003.
- It is 45 in the REM 0094 FHN witness and 27 in the Millet witness.
- These 178 tokens belong to 73 distinct forms.
- This retest judged 41 of the 73 forms.
- It tested the top 20 forms first, then 21 more.
- One gloss held up: penn, meaning year.
- No gloss was refuted.
- Forty forms stayed untested.
- Twelve of the forty hold no candidate meaning at all.
- The other twenty-eight hold a meaning but too little evidence.

## Verification

- Old gap tokens were 1535, and new gap tokens are 1357.
- The difference is 178.
- Per narrative the drops are 505 to 440, 326 to 285, 159 to 114, and 545 to 518.
- Those drops are 65, 41, 45, and 27.
- The gap-list diff gives the same four numbers.
- It also gives 73 distinct newly covered forms.
- Three spot checks passed.
- The 0104 translation reads "cush-wards to adere, northwards to philae".
- Its text puts mtr after each place name.
- The 0145 translation lists "1 bXe of the yed" among donated goods.
- The U3-11 file records six supports for penn, with the same six inscriptions.

## The one supported gloss

- Penn means year in the Rilly gloss "lever (d'un astre); annee".
- Six inscriptions support the year sense.
- They are OTTEN-MILLET-0055, OTTEN-MILLET-0150, and REM 0089.
- They are also OTTEN-MILLET-0341, OTTEN-MILLET-0353, and OTTEN-MILLET-0354.
- The support comes from U3-11, not from new evidence.
- The merge added only REM 0094 tokens for penn.
- Those tokens sit in "plw penn tlte", which no translation covers.
- The star-rising sense has no context anywhere.
- It stays untested.

## No candidate meaning

- Twelve judged forms hold no candidate meaning.
- Ten merged entries carry no gloss at all.
- They are amnp, dete, kek, kete, tepke, tmot, wit, lks, mnitke, and mse.
- Eqe has only garbled discussion text.
- Rilly says its sense stays uncertain.
- Simlok matches a stem whose gloss names a place.
- That gloss is not a meaning for simlok.

## Why so much stays untested

- Most new forms occur in only one inscription.
- The royal narratives give summaries, not word-by-word translations.
- No translation covers most new tokens.
- Position alone rarely decides a meaning.
- The mtr case shows the limit clearly.
- All six mtr inscriptions quote one REM 0094 passage.
- One passage cannot carry a verdict.

## Limits

- The penn support predates the merge.
- It comes from U3-11, and the merge added nothing to it.
- Thirty-two newly covered forms remain unjudged.
- Three of them hold glosses: qorose, seleleke, and seleleyte.
- No gloss was upgraded to certain.
- Every tested gloss stays proposed.
